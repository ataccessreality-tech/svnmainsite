Layout Approval and RERA Registration: Two Different Checks

Why buyers should compare the approved plan and applicable RERA disclosures for the exact phase and plot.

Approvals · 2 min read · 11 October 2026

In this article
  1. Begin with the exact property
  2. What the approved plan helps you check
  3. What RERA disclosures add
  4. Permission to build is another question
  5. Keep a dated verification record

Begin with the exact property

An approval reference is only useful when you can connect it to the property being offered. Ask for the project name, promoter, phase, survey references and plot or unit number. Use the same identifiers in your quotation, document folder and site notes.

A project can contain different phases or parcels. Do not assume that a document displayed for one part automatically covers every plot shown in a presentation. Ask for a written explanation of the scope when the match is unclear.

What the approved plan helps you check

A layout plan can show the approved arrangement of plots, roads, open spaces and other designated areas. Check its issuing authority, reference, date and conditions, then locate your plot and compare its dimensions with the sale plan.

Ask whether later revisions exist and which version applies. If a road, amenity area or plot boundary differs between the brochure and approved drawing, ask for clarification before treating the brochure as a specification.

What RERA disclosures add

Where the RERA framework applies, use the official state regulator to compare the project, promoter, location and registration information. Review the disclosed material and available updates rather than checking only whether a number appears on an advertisement.

Registration and planning permission serve different purposes. Neither removes the need to check title, seller authority and the contract. If registration is not provided, ask for the written basis and obtain advice on applicability rather than accepting an unexplained exemption claim.

Permission to build is another question

Buying a residential plot does not automatically approve your particular house design. Building controls, setbacks, site conditions, service arrangements and applicable permissions still need to be addressed.

If the parcel was used or classified as agricultural land, clarify the current land-use and conversion requirements with the competent authority. Procedures can change, and rules from another state may not fit an Andhra Pradesh property.

Keep a dated verification record

Save the official records you reviewed and write down the date and unanswered questions. If an important permission is only promised, describe it as pending in your comparison and ask what commitment the contract actually makes.

The free documents handbook provides a review checklist. Use it with a lawyer and the relevant local professionals so each document answers a specific question about the property you intend to buy.
