The family

Three generations of leadership

The Gudisa family has led SVN ShivaJyothi Group across three generations, from 1971 to today.

Three generations, one name

The SVN family enterprise began with Gudisa Venkateswara Rao in 1971. Gudisa Shiva Kumar carried it into real estate in 2003. Today, the third generation includes Gudisa Nithin in construction and real estate operations, and Gudisa Yashwanth in hotel management.
