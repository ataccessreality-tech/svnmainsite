The record

A legacy built on purpose, since 1971

SVN ShivaJyothi Group traces its origins to 1971. This is the group's year-by-year record of 19 milestones, from the first venture to the current projects.

The record, 1971 to 2026

The SVN story begins in 1971, when Lt. Gudisa Venkateswara Rao founded the family enterprise. The group entered real estate in 2003. The timeline below lists each milestone in the group's own record.

  1. 1971
    The Beginning

    The family enterprise is founded

    Lt. G. Venkateswara Rao sets down the first foundation of the family enterprise in North Andhra.

  2. 1983
    First Venture

    SVN Color Lab

    The family name enters trade, building a reputation for precision and service.

  3. 2003
    The Real-Estate Chapter

    SVN ShivaJyothi Group · SVN Nagar

    The group formally enters real estate. SVN Nagar, a twenty-five-acre layout, carries the family name onto the map.

  4. 2005
    Devotion

    Gnana Saraswathi Devi Temple

    A temple commission; the group builds for the community, not only the market.

  5. 2006
    Residential

    Shivajyothi Enclave · 20 Flats

    An early residential enclave deepens the housing portfolio.

  6. 2007
    Hospitality

    Hotel SVN Lake Palace

    The group enters hospitality, extending the SVN name to leisure.

  7. 2008
    Residential

    Sirisha Towers · 20 Flats

    A residential tower rises as the corridor densifies.

  8. 2009
    Devotion

    Dwadasa Jyotirlinga Temple

    A second temple, reinforcing the family’s civic and spiritual commitments.

  9. 2010
    Layout · 8 Acres

    Kasturi Nagar

    An eight-acre layout expands the residential footprint.

  10. 2013
    Hospitality · Kakinada

    Hotel SVN Grand

    A grand hotel in Kakinada widens the group’s hospitality reach.

  11. 2013
    Commercial

    Pasupathi Plaza

    The group moves into commercial development.

  12. 2014
    Residential · 70 Flats

    Pasupathi Avishkar

    Seventy flats; the residential portfolio reaches new scale.

  13. 2015
    Layout · 16 Acres

    Pasupathi Nagar

    A sixteen-acre layout, one of the group’s largest to date.

  14. 2016
    Layout · 2 Acres

    Lakshmi Ganapathi Nagar

    A compact, considered layout continues the corridor’s growth.

  15. 2019
    Layout · 3 Acres

    SVN 83° East

    A three-acre layout named for its coordinate on the coast.

  16. 2020
    Layout · 5 Acres

    SVN Goldcrest

    A five-acre layout adds to the growing corridor portfolio.

  17. 2022
    The Coastal Destination

    SVN Bay Parck Beach Resort

    Forty-five acres on the Bay of Bengal. The flagship, and the group’s largest destination yet.

  18. 2025
    Diversification

    Swadya Gourmet Catering

    The family name enters gourmet catering.

  19. 2026
    The Next Chapter

    SVN Parckwud, miraya BY SVN and Ayodhara

    Three new communities join SVN Bay Parck, carrying the family name along the Visakhapatnam and Bhogapuram corridor.
