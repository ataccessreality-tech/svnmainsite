Privacy Policy

How SVN ShivaJyothi Group collects and uses information submitted through this website.

Draft text. This page is held back from search indexing until it has been reviewed by a qualified legal professional.

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry we collect your name, email address, phone number, the project you are interested in and your information preferences. We also record the page you enquired from, how you reached the site (referrer and campaign parameters) and the time of submission.

Why we collect it

To respond to your enquiry, arrange site visits and share information about our projects, only where you have given consent.

WhatsApp

If you choose to continue on WhatsApp, your enquiry text is sent through WhatsApp under its own terms.

Analytics and cookies

When enabled, we use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. This may set cookies in your browser. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent, by writing to info@svnsjd.com.
