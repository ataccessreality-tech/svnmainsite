SVN Bay Parck Beach Resort

SVN Bay Parck Beach Resort is a forty-five-acre coastal development on the Bay of Bengal at Jeerupalem, Ranastalam, Srikakulam district, Andhra Pradesh, by SVN ShivaJyothi Group.

Overview

SVN Bay Parck Beach Resort is a forty-five-acre coastal development on the Bay of Bengal at Jeerupalem, Ranastalam, Srikakulam district, Andhra Pradesh, by SVN ShivaJyothi Group.

It is the group's largest destination to date, begun in 2022.
